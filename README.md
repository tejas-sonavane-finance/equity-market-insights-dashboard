# Equity Market Insights Dashboard

Multi-tier Tableau dashboard analyzing **$262T in global market capitalization** across **5 major indices** (S&P 500, Nifty 50, DAX 40, Nikkei 225, Hang Seng) from 2020–2024, applying a **reverse pyramid framework** drilling from global markets → sectors → industries → top 10 companies by market cap.

## 🔺 Reverse Pyramid Framework

| Level | Scope | Question Answered |
|---|---|---|
| **Global** | 5 indices, $262T market cap | Which markets are strongest? |
| **Sector** | 11 GICS sectors per index | Which sectors deliver best returns? |
| **Industry** | Top 10 industries by market cap | Which industries lead profitability and capital efficiency? |
| **Company** | Top 10 companies per index | Which specific companies outperform? |

## 📑 5-Page Structure

1. **Cover** — Index overview and median ROE comparison
2. **Global Level** — KPI cards, market cap trends, fundamentals heat map
3. **Industry Level** — Revenue vs profitability, market cap share, capital intensity scatter
4. **Sector Level** — Sector returns, S&P 500 deep dive, growth leaders treemap
5. **Company Level** — Top 10 treemap, ROE comparison, EBITDA & revenue growth

## 🎯 Key Findings

- **Information Technology** led global growth at **24.69% CAGR**
- **Pharmaceuticals** highest profitability at **32.52% EBITDA margin**
- **Semiconductors** ranked second at **32.42% EBITDA margin**
- **Nifty 50 (India)** delivered the highest median ROE at **14.40%**, above S&P 500 (14.10%)
- Top decile companies consistently outperform industry-average ROE — validates concentrated mega-cap allocation
